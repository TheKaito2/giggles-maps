Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Sign in for 3 detours a day, or watch a short ad for one more. Membership removes the limit.

How does check-in work? What about my photos?

At the stop, take any photo; GPS confirms you're inside the place's check-in zone (50 m, or 150 m for landmarks and parks). Your photos go into your passport album, which your friends can see, and so can people on a ride-along trip with you for photos from that trip. A photo you share on a place's page is public. We never analyse faces. Photos of check-ins that weren't accepted are deleted after 30 days; the rest are kept until you delete your account (or email us to remove one).

What does "Partner" mean?

A shop that pays ฿299 a month. It shows first on members' routes and always carries a Partner label. Giggles Choice partners are hand-picked by application.

Which areas does it cover?

All 77 provinces of Thailand, with more than 110,000 places.
