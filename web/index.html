How it works

  1. 1

    Set your extra time

    Start, destination, and the minutes you'll allow on top. 0 to 120.

  2. 2

    Get a route that fits

    Real walking or driving times. Best stops first, open when you arrive. Your limit is a hard limit.

  3. 3

    Check in, collect stamps

    A selfie plus GPS stamps your passport and gives you a free coupon spin. Finish a trail to earn the badge.

The route is never for sale.

Quality first. Stops are picked by rating, variety and how well they fit your extra time. Temples, parks and free spots make the cut on merit.

One Partner, at most. A paying shop can only fill minutes you have left over, never push out a better free stop, and is always labelled Partner.

Relevant, not loudest. The café next to a stop already on your route beats a bigger bid across town.

For shops

Pay only when a traveller is at your counter.

A visit counts when the traveller scans your counter QR sticker, is within 60 m, takes a selfie, and saw your shop on their route in the last 3 hours. Once per person per 30 days. Hide the sticker and you stop getting traffic, so nobody games it.
